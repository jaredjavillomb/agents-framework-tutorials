# ShipFast Support Policy

Today's date for all policy calculations is **2026-11-01**.

## P1. Lost packages
If the carrier status is `lost`, the customer is entitled to a full refund of the order total. No further checks are needed.

## P2. Delayed packages
A package is "late" when today is more than 5 days past its ETA.
- Late, and the customer asks to cancel or for a refund: issue a full refund of the order total.
- Late, and the customer does not ask for a refund: apologise, give the latest tracking event, and offer a 15% store credit.
- Not late (5 days or fewer past ETA, or ETA in the future): no refund. Give the latest tracking event and the ETA.

## P3. Damaged items
Damage must be reported within 7 days of the delivery date, and the customer must provide a photo.
- No photo yet: ask the customer for a photo. Do not refund.
- Photo provided and within 7 days: issue a full refund of the order total.
- Reported more than 7 days after delivery: not eligible; offer to escalate.

## P4. Refund window
No refunds for orders delivered more than 30 days ago, for any reason. Politely decline and offer to escalate to a human agent.

## P5. Approval threshold
Any refund **over $100.00** must be approved by a human supervisor before it is issued.
- Approved: issue the refund.
- Rejected: do not refund; escalate to a human agent and tell the customer a specialist will follow up.

## P6. Unknown orders
If an order ID is not found, ask the customer to double-check the order ID. Never guess.

## P7. Scope
Only handle orders, deliveries, refunds and complaints. Politely decline anything else.
